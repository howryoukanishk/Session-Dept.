philosophy

slowing down, in style.

session dept. is a design project focused on sensory objects and premium smoking accessories for a modern, creative generation.

manifesto v1.0

we believe in reclaiming the pause.

In a hyper-digitized, fast-paced world, taking a break has become transactional. We smoke, we scroll, we rush. session dept. was founded to challenge this culture of constant movement.

Our tools are designed to look striking in your living space and feel substantial in your hands. By focusing on heavy stoneware ceramics, unrefined natural fibers, and minimal geometries, we invite you to put down the screen, light up, slow down, and stay in session.

our story

Kanishk Prabhat

Founder & Creative Director

Originally hailing from Bihar, Kanishk grew up surrounded by a landscape of rich cultural craft, architectural ruins, and an unhurried, slow-paced lifestyle. These formative years instilled in him a deep respect for raw textures, handmade earthen materials, and the beauty of conscious pauses.

Now living and working in New Delhi, Kanishk bridges the calm nostalgia of his roots with the sharp, high-contrast, energetic aesthetics of urban Gen Z design. With session dept., he fuses sculptural design with everyday smoking essentials.

"We don't design utility objects; we design the space and state of mind around them. A break is merely time passing. A session is a conscious ritual."

Every curved silhouette, raw clay finish, and design drop is curated personally under Kanishk's vision to make premium accessories accessible for contemporary creatives.

our design pillars

01 / tactile presence

We select materials—heavy stone ceramics, matte borosilicate glass, organic hemp—that demand touch. Moving away from plastic and shiny metals towards grounding textures.

02 / bold contrast

Brutalist layout weight, raw typography, signature vibrant lime accents, and warm vintage cream colors. Designing pieces that stand out as art objects on your table.

03 / intentional rhythm

Our products and streams promote a lifestyle of pacing oneself. Soundtracks, accessories, and kits designed to stretch a five-minute break into a true slow session.
