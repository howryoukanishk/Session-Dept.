What is session dept.?

session dept. is a boutique design project and premium accessories studio focusing on stoneware ceramic ashtrays, organic raw rolling papers, and curated session kits for a creative generation. Fusing editorial aesthetics with functional design, we build pieces intended to stand out in your space. Established in 2026, we run office hubs in New Delhi and Patna, Bihar.

What are your shipping rates and times across India?

We provide complimentary standard shipping on all orders throughout India. We process and dispatch orders within 24-48 hours. Depending on your location (Delhi NCR, Patna, or other tier-1/tier-2 hubs), standard transit delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. A tracking link is dispatched immediately upon order confirmation.

How do I clean and maintain my ceramic ashtrays?

Our stoneware ceramic ashtrays are crafted from durable high-fire clay and protected by a robust glaze layer, making them completely dishwasher safe. For hand washing, we recommend using warm water, standard dishwashing liquid, and a soft sponge. Refrain from using coarse wire scrubbers or heavy abrasives, as they can wear down the signature satin glaze finish over time.

What makes your rolling papers unrefined and natural?

Our sessions papers are made from 100% organic, unbleached, and unrefined hemp fibers, maintaining their natural earth-brown shade without the use of chlorine, chalk, or chemical colorings. They utilize a premium, plant-based gum Arabic adhesive line. This combination ensures a slow, even burn rate that doesn't interfere with the natural flavor profile of your blend.

Do you offer wholesale accounts for lounges or boutiques?

Yes. We provide wholesale pricing rates and bulk packaging arrangements for design boutiques, concept stores, and lounge accounts. To apply, fill out the form on our contact page, select "wholesale inquiry" as the subject, or email our licensing and sales desk directly at wholesale@sessiondept.com.

Why do you have a 21+ verification gate on the website?

We believe in compliance and age verification standards. Since we distribute functional smoking accessories, collection bongs, and rolling papers, visitors must verify they are 21 years of age or older. We store a non-tracking, local validation state to prevent prompting verified devices repeatedly.
